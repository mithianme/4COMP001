# Research: Why students need this app
Found by Glenn

## Fact 1
- What it says: Of the students who get a student loan, 58% said it doesn't cover their living costs. Another 25% said it covers them, but only just.
- Who published it: Office for National Statistics (ONS)
- Year: 2023 (survey of 1,964 higher education students in England, 30 January to 13 February 2023)
- Link: https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/educationandchildcare/bulletins/costoflivingandhighereducationstudentsengland/30januaryto13february2023

## Fact 2
- What it says: 49% of students said they were having financial difficulties. 30% had taken on new debt because of the rising cost of living, and 71% of those said they did it because their loan wasn't enough to live on.
- Who published it: Office for National Statistics (ONS)
- Year: 2023 (same survey as Fact 1)
- Link: https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/educationandchildcare/bulletins/costoflivingandhighereducationstudentsengland/30januaryto13february2023

## Fact 3
- What it says: The average student spends about £1,142 a month, but the average maintenance loan works out at about £640 a month. That leaves them roughly £500 short every month.
- Who published it: Save the Student, National Student Money Survey 2025
- Year: 2025 (the loan figure is for students from England in 2024/25)
- Link: https://www.savethestudent.org/money/surveys/student-money-survey-2025-results.html

## Why students would want our app
Students run out of money before their next loan payment comes in, and it is usually caused by small things such as: a meal deal, a bus fare or a night out. None of it feels like much on the day, but overtime it slowly builds up. Banking apps show you a list of what you've spent, but they don't tell you how much you've got left for the week. Our app you can set a weekly budget, see what's left as soon as you open it, and get a warning before you go over.
